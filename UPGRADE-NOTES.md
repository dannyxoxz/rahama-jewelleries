# Rahama Essentials — Slideshow Edition

This edition replaces the previous 3D hero with a responsive luxury product slideshow.

- Friendly homepage headline: “Jewellery for your special moments.”
- Warm obsidian, champagne-gold and cream visual direction; no purple hero styling.
- Product slides are sourced from the existing catalogue images.
- Automatic playback, pause/play, next/previous controls, slide indicators and mobile swipe gestures.
- Mobile-first sizing and touch-friendly controls.
- Existing catalogue, cart, admin, backend and WhatsApp enquiry flow are preserved.

The 3D viewer and Blender build folder have been removed from this edition by design.
